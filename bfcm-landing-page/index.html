From 17TRACK’s global logistics data: what December does to Black Friday orders.

  • 2–3×

    Return volume peaks in December

    The 30 days after Black Friday are the year’s highest return peak. Requests can reach 2–3× a normal day.

  • 30%+

    Refunds that can be kept

    More than 30% of peak-season returns can stay as revenue through exchanges and store credit.

  • Minutes

    Review, automated
